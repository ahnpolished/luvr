# Luvr — Design Brief

## What is Luvr?

Luvr is an AI dating advice chatbot that lives in your messaging apps. You text it like a friend — questions, screenshots of confusing conversations, voice memos about a date that went sideways — and it gives you honest, practical advice back.

Think: a wise, non-judgmental friend who happens to know a lot about relationships. Not a therapist. Not an app with a dashboard. Just a text conversation.

Currently: Telegram. Coming soon: iMessage.

## Who uses it?

People actively navigating dating — someone figuring out whether to text back, someone wanting a reality check on a red flag, someone venting after a weird date. Likely 20s–early 30s, used to texting as their primary communication mode.

## Tone

**Warm but honest.** Luvr doesn't just validate — it gives real advice even when it's a little uncomfortable. No toxic positivity, no vague affirmations.

**Conversational, not clinical.** Responses feel like texting a smart friend, not reading a self-help article. Short paragraphs. Plain language. No bulleted lists of "5 steps to better communication."

**Grounded, not corny.** It doesn't say things like "you deserve love!" or "what's your love language?" It talks about real situations in plain terms.

**Non-judgmental by default.** All orientations, relationship styles, and situations are welcome. It meets people where they are.

## What it is NOT

- Not a matchmaking app (no profiles, no swiping)
- Not a journaling or self-help platform
- Not a social network
- Not a therapist replacement
- Not cute or playful in an infantilizing way

## Key interactions to design for

1. **Onboarding web flow** — sign up via phone number, quick context collection (minimal: under 4 steps), link to Telegram
2. **Bot conversation** — primarily handled in Telegram; design focus here is branding, not UI chrome
3. **Web account page** — eventually: usage, settings, profile context

## Name & brand notes

"Luvr" — intentional misspelling, suggests something casual and a little cheeky without being cringe. The misspelling signals it's not corporate. Don't over-explain it.

Tagline direction: *"Like texting a friend who actually knows what they're talking about."*
